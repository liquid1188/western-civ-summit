# Design notes. International Summit for Western Civilization

Read this before changing the site. It keeps yearly updates consistent.

## What the site is for
One job: get the right people to request a place. Visitors are legislators, officials, clergy, scholars, and press.
It should feel serious, classical, and welcoming.

## Type
- Display and body headings: Cormorant Garamond.
- Labels and small caps: Cinzel, letterspaced. Labels only, never paragraphs.
- Text: Source Sans 3.

## Color
- Ink `#0a101d` and navy `#111b30` for dark bands and headings.
- Marble `#f5f0e6` and paper `#fffdf8` for light sections.
- Gold `#c8a45a` for rules and accents. Dark gold `#7a5f22` for small labels on light backgrounds.
- Porphyry `#74202c` for emphasis words. Use sparingly.

## The theme
Athens, Rome, Jerusalem. Three, never four. It is woven into real content:
- The three columns in The Occasion that join into Natural Law.
- The Voices section: one quotation each from Athens (Aristotle), Rome (Cicero), Jerusalem (Genesis).
It does not get a showcase section of portraits or paintings.

## Layout rules
- Do not center every section. Purpose and Vision is heading left, text right. Objectives is left-aligned.
- Lists that sit side by side have the same number of lines.
- Names never break badly. No single word alone on a line.
- On phones the hero painting sits above the title, not behind it.
- The same picture is never used twice on one page.
- Motion is limited to: hero words rising, sections revealing in order, the progress line, pointer responses. Do not add more.

## Features to keep
- The countdown.
- "Request to Attend" wording.
- The phone bar with the request button.
- Add to calendar links.

## Do not
- Reuse features from the ICJF site (rolling logo band, banner).
- Add a city to the triad.
- Put hotel or venue details up before the venue is confirmed.

## Each year
Add a new file in `src/summits/` and change `currentYear` in `src/_data/site.json`. Past years move to Speeches & Media on their own.
